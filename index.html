About the candidate

A practising advocate
standing for the women
of this bar

Neha Chauhan practises at Karkardooma Court, Delhi, appearing before the District Courts and allied forums in civil, criminal, matrimonial and consumer matters. She holds an MBA alongside her LLB, and runs her chamber at E-70, First Floor, within the court complex.

She is contesting the Shahdara Bar Association election for the post of Lady Member Executive for the term 2027–2028, on a platform built around the day-to-day working conditions of women advocates — facilities, safety, and a genuine seat at the table when decisions are taken.

Members are welcome at the chamber on any working day to raise a concern, seek assistance with a matter, or simply have a conversation about what this bar should look like.

The agenda

Six boxes worth ticking

Practical commitments for the 2027–2028 term — the things members actually raise at the bar room, put down in writing.

Facilities for women advocates

Clean and secure washrooms, a dedicated ladies' common room with lockers, and a functioning crèche for members with young children.

Support for juniors

A structured mentorship register, seating in the bar room for juniors, and assistance for first-year enrolments with chamber placement.

Welfare and health cover

Wider participation in the advocates' welfare fund, group medical cover, and timely disbursal of assistance in cases of illness or bereavement.

Safety and grievance redressal

A standing internal committee that meets on a fixed date, transparent minutes, and a confidential channel for complaints of harassment.

Library and digital access

An updated bar library with current reporters, e-journal subscriptions, working Wi-Fi in the bar room, and cause lists on a members' group.

Everyday court conditions

Drinking water and hygienic canteen standards, orderly parking for members, and follow-up with the administration on lifts, ramps and seating.
